# Cloudiator cosmetic payments

**Status:** Avi approved the design and requested the [PayPal sandbox implementation plan](../plans/2026-11-08-cosmetic-payments-paypal-sandbox.md) on 2026-11-08. This is a separate release from Patch 1.8 and M31.

## First paid release

Avi chose two one-time, account-bound cosmetics for the first release. The **Supporter Banner** is a distinct cosmetic with its own art and ownership. Founder remains a separate title and is unavailable through purchases. The **pet follower** is a visual companion with zero gameplay bonuses. Weapon and armor dyes and skins are follow-ups. Gold, renown, gear power, subscriptions, and trading paid items are outside this release.

The proposed shop lists the two items separately, each with a price, currency and purchase status. Players can equip an owned banner or pet from My Gladiator and remove it again. The pet needs approved pixel art and movement that fits camp paths. Its animation is presentation only. A verified full refund takes the affected paid item out of use, including when equipped. Show a clickable desktop and mobile mockup over real Cloudiator screenshots for Avi’s approval before implementing player-facing UI.

## Provider options

**Proposed first sandbox:** PayPal Business for the Israeli seller, using PayPal-hosted checkout. PayPal offers Business accounts in Israel and a sandbox for testing the flow from checkout to item delivery. Confirm eligibility for these virtual goods, supported currencies and buyer payment methods, payouts, fees, and merchant responsibilities for the actual seller account before choosing a live provider. [PayPal Business Israel](https://www.paypal.com/il/business) · [PayPal sandbox](https://developer.paypal.com/sandbox-testing/overview)

**Alternative:** Xsolla offers a games-focused merchant-of-record arrangement. Consider it if the seller wants that arrangement and Xsolla confirms onboarding for the Israeli entity, these products, and the desired markets. Confirm terms directly. Stripe’s public country list currently omits Israel for direct seller onboarding. [Xsolla Payments](https://xsolla.com/payments) · [Stripe global availability](https://stripe.com/global)

## Payment and ownership

1. Keep Cloudflare Pages static. A Supabase Edge checkout function verifies the signed-in account, checks it has a gladiator, accepts a product identifier, and creates or resumes an order for that account. The server owns the product, price, currency, provider and return origin. Use one item per order and reuse an open order for the same account and item. The order stores a price and currency snapshot. Provider create and capture requests use stable idempotency keys for retries. Keep provider credentials in server secrets. The browser redirects to the provider-hosted approval page and never handles card data. [PayPal Orders v2](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/api/orders/v2/)
2. After buyer approval, the backend captures the known order. Returning to the game shows status from the server. A separate Edge webhook verifies PayPal’s signature against the configured app and webhook before accepting an event. Grant only after the backend verifies a completed capture linked to the recorded account order, matching the expected provider order, payee, product reference, amount and currency. Reconcile orders left pending if the buyer closes the page or a delivery fails. [PayPal Webhooks v1](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/api/webhooks/v1/) · [PayPal Payments v2](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/api/payments/v2/) · [Supabase Edge Function auth](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/auth#external-webhooks)
3. Keep orders and event records private. In one database transaction, record the event, update the order, and grant or revoke the entitlement. Uniqueness on provider event, order and capture IDs plus one active entitlement per account and item prevents duplicate fulfillment. Lock order transitions so late events cannot restore a revoked grant. Reconcile against provider state, including duplicate charges, and only revoke for the affected purchase. Proposed handling is automatic revocation for a verified full refund or final reversal, with partial refunds and open disputes sent for operator review. Equip and display checks must honor current entitlement state.
4. Use a dedicated paid entitlement ledger. The existing `owned_cosmetics` rows are immutable and limited to the in-game `banner` catalog item. Paid ownership has a separate item ID and lifecycle, with no effect on the existing banner or title triggers. Expose only an owner’s own purchase status and entitlements via restricted endpoints or owner-only row policies. Camp may read the public equipped appearance needed to render the cosmetic. It does not expose payment records or metadata. [Existing banner schema](../../../supabase/sql/04-inventory-shops.sql)

## Verification and launch

Prove success, cancel and pending in the sandbox for both items. Cover spoofed returns, tampered signatures, wrong payee or amount, currency mismatches, concurrent and out-of-order events, dropped deliveries, refunds and revocation of an equipped item, account isolation, and cosmetic-only behavior. Keep sandbox and live orders, credentials and endpoints separate. Leave checkout disabled by default and keep processing earlier payments and refunds if new checkout is paused. Follow the normal database, Edge and browser release gates. Avi’s exact approval is required for each production write and for enabling real-money checkout.

Open decisions are each item’s price and sale currency, the first pet species and look, and the provider and seller arrangements for live launch. Confirm the refund and support process, receipts and tax treatment for the actual seller before enabling checkout.
