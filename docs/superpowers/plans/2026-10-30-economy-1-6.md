# Patch 1.6, the Economy Rebalance: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Execution:** subagent-driven (superpowers:subagent-driven-development), already chosen by Avi. **The release needs Avi's
go in chat** for every production write (Task 10). **The warning message is posted a day ahead** of the switch, with Avi's
go on its filled-in text (Task 10, Step 3). The last task (Task 11) makes a players' clip; **Avi's patch-clip rule**
applies: the reply that reports 1.6 live reminds him of the clip, names the patch and the features since the last video's
patch (CHANGELOG.md, "Videos"), and offers to make it, with a feature post for the WhatsApp game group and a YouTube post;
no paid render, upload or post without his go.

**Goal:** Ship Avi's economy decisions as patch 1.6: a new rules version `v2` (XP × 0.3, expedition gold 4 / 10 / 18,
salvage 4 gold, gear found half as often, a Training cap of 10 + 4 × level, tournament and season gold by league), started
by one owner-only switch that also cuts every wallet to a quarter, plus the Arena banner on the profile card.

**Architecture:** Pure, versioned TypeScript decides every amount: an immutable `RULES_V2` beside the unchanged
`RULES_V1`, and one `economy.ts` that is the only place knowing how they differ. The worker registers executors for both
versions, so each job is paid by the version of the boundary that froze it. One migration, M27 from
`supabase/sql/21-economy.sql`, seeds `v2` inactive, publishes the active version on the revision read, stamps each new
season with its version, pays season prizes by version and league, publishes the banner, and adds the switch
`private.switch_to_economy_v2()`. The client reads the active version from the revision poll to word the Schedule, the
prizes and salvage values.

**Tech Stack:** Deno Edge functions (`game-command`, `game-worker`), PostgreSQL / Supabase CLI 2.120.0, Next.js 16.4.3
static client, React, Vitest, Playwright (`channel: 'chrome'`, private config).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-economy-1-6-design.md` (Avi's decisions, the exact numbers, `RULES_V2`, the
switch, every word of player-facing copy). The economy proposal it settles is local:
`.superpowers/design/2026-10-29-economy/proposal.md`.

   converts overflowing salvage at 10; a v1 Training credits its point even above the cap; the next boundary is v2. Task 5

   never throws; a player far above the cap (94 points at level 1) reads "until level 22"; a visitor's season table marks
