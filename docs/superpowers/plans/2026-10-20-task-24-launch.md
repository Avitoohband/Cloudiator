## Review Focus

- **Deploy order on a live database.** A client or Edge function ahead of its schema breaks the app (`readRevision` needs M18's column; `presence` needs M22); a CRLF `db push` changes function bodies; a migration that replays locally can fail under the hosted, non-superuser `postgres` (for example M01:558 `ALTER PUBLICATION supabase_realtime OWNER`). Test: 24.4 (dry run, then function-body fingerprint parity) and the bench rehearsal.
- **Hosted exposure differs from local.** Exposed schemas (`graphql_public`, `private`), `max_rows`, email or anonymous sign-up left on, platform default ACLs. Test: 24.4's access-audit diff, plus `GET /auth/v1/settings` and anon RPC and write-denial probes in 24.6.
- **The real origin breaks sign-in or commands.** The hosted gateway rewriting `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` to `*`; `verify_jwt = true` on `game-command` refusing preflights or new-format JWTs; a redirect allow-list missing the trailing slash (`client.ts:26` sends `/auth/callback/`); a Google consent screen left in "Testing". Test: 24.5 probes and 24.8 sign-in on desktop and phone.
- **Launch activation.** The epoch set in the past or after members exist, or the scheduler enabled before the Vault secrets exist, stalls boundaries (`wait:'epoch'`, `unconfigured`). Test: 24.7, and a real boundary published within 120 s with `duplicateGrants: 0` in 24.8.
- **Free-tier limits.** Hosted worker CPU and wall time against 2,000 ms; at most 5 dispatches per scheduled minute; uncompressed or Edge-to-API-billed egress (raw with backend traffic measured at 113–116 % locally); invocations including preflights; project pausing. Test: 24.9.
