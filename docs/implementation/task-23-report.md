## What Task 23 delivers

**Security hardening and the access matrix.** M17 revokes every client privilege beyond SELECT and schema USAGE that the matrix found: anon and authenticated MAINTAIN on the eight M01 owner tables, authenticated MAINTAIN, REFERENCES, TRIGGER and TRUNCATE on `owner_state`, and the postgres default privileges that gave clients sequence UPDATE and table TRUNCATE, REFERENCES, TRIGGER and MAINTAIN on future objects (30 audit rows). `tests/db/access-matrix.test.ts` checks every role and privilege kind from the catalog: RLS on every public table, `security_invoker` on every view, no SECURITY DEFINER, a pinned `search_path`, no client EXECUTE or default privilege, nothing for clients on new objects, and a pinned service_role set. It attempts real denied writes and RPCs as the owner, another account and a visitor, proves seeds, plans and snapshots unreadable even by their owner, and proves no account reads another owner's private state, with a per-relation anchor. `scripts/access-audit.sql` is the same read-only query for the hosted comparison in the runbook; its policy rows carry an md5 of their expressions. service_role's broader backend privileges (D) and the platform defaults (E) are documented exceptions. The static export ships a generated `_headers` with Decision 7's CSP and security headers (`connect-src` limited to the build's Supabase origin), the art gallery is removed, the scanner refuses any JWT and base64-encoded `service_role`, every browser test fails on a CSP violation, and `npm audit` found 0 vulnerabilities in 152 dependencies.

**Timing.** Every stable-slot wait in the DB tests times out no earlier than its longest sleep plus its own work, enforced by a static check. The worker retries a busy coordination after its own work up to three times, 250–499 ms apart. The dispatch budget is counted per scheduled minute (5 a minute), and the public revision state carries `published_slot_index`, which the client uses instead of the five-minute "being prepared" grace. Held-window publication fell from 121.3 s to 61.4 s at production cadence.

**Capacity measurement and the M19/M20 fixes.** `node scripts/resource-report.mjs` runs a real 50-character world through the real tick and Edge worker, sizes every read through the app's adapters, and projects the database, egress and invocations against the budgets; `CAPACITY_GATE=1` makes `test:db` enforce them. The run found boundary publication at 121.6 s after any expedition slot. M19 dispatches and claims settlements together with the due reveals that release them, and M20 leases a reveal that several settlements share once, before them: publication now takes 61.5–61.6 s in every window, with zero headroom at 50 all-expedition characters (a named risk). In a 31-day month: raw client egress 79.0 % (gzip 19.0 %), 115.9 % with the backend's own traffic (a Task 24 question); invocations 14.3 % (84.7 % at the dispatch ceiling); worker CPU at most 9.3 % of its budget on this machine; season close and standings well under 1 %.

**Read and render cost.** A 64-entry least-recently-used tournament log cache; the camp pauses under an opaque scene (7,852 draw calls a second before, none after); "being prepared" re-reads back off after three minutes; ring order no longer depends on arrival order, and failed ring logs retry by real time; owner, `/replay/` and Arena reads are de-duplicated (8 Arena timeline reads instead of 13); a camp Reload and Try again on failed replays and tournament logs; an opt-in `?perf=1` overlay. Desktop holds the 170 Hz vsync ceiling with no frame over 16.7 ms in the camp phases; mobile 30 fps is unmeasured (Task 24).

**Accessibility.** The skip link is the first Tab stop on every route; the focus never stays on `<body>` (a focus rescue, and the first-visit creation dialog hands the focus to the chip); validation messages are linked to their fields with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` in Build, Schedule, Shop, Market and Create; a trade's outcome takes the focus after a keyboard command only; contrast meets 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for controls (a new `--field-line`); accessibility snapshots pin the rail, a menu frame, the scene, the creation dialog, the purchase confirmation and the profile card; a bout has one end definition; the scene history and remount races are fixed. Reduced motion stays OS-driven.

**The release gate and CI.** `npm run verify` runs every gate in order (unit, typecheck, assets, build, the static scan, the Edge settle, `CAPACITY_GATE=1 npm run test:db`, the development browsers, the isolated production build, its scan, the production browsers and `git diff --check`), runs every step whose input is valid after a failure, and exits 0, 2 (named known blockers only; the list is empty since 23.8) or 1. A host network fault is labelled in the summary and never rerun. Before the database tests the gate refreshes the Edge source's last-access times and waits for a settled runtime (C22: NTFS last-access updates had been recreating the Edge container mid-run). `.github/workflows/checks.yml` runs the gate on a fresh stack with SHA-pinned actions; it has not run yet.

**The database budget.** On Avi's decision (A + C + D, 2026-10-19), completed jobs' payloads are emptied once their slot has settled (M21; a job row goes from 2,109 B to 867 B), and the internal budget is 500 MB with a health warning at 400 MB (`budgetBytes` 524,288,000, read against the physical size); the health report also counts `cohort.activeCharacters`. At 90 days the worst case is 82.0 % (mixed schedules) and 69.2 % (new-character schedule) of 500 MB; after a year it is 129.9 % and 107.9 %, a named risk. The structural compactions are built after launch, when the warning appears.

**Idle rest and presence.** A character whose owner has sent no activity signal for 14 days, measured from each boundary's scheduled start, rests at home (M22): it has no cohort member, job, snapshot, activity, settled slot or season rating row, while its processed slot advances. Every command records the owner's signal at most once an hour, and the client sends a `presence` command once when the gladiator is shown and every 6 hours while visible; the character returns at the next boundary, with no back-fill. A resting gladiator is not drawn in the camp. Others read "Resting at home: back when {name}'s owner returns."; the owner reads "Resting at home: back on your schedule at {time}". The health report counts `cohort.restingCharacters`. Spec §4.3 changed with Avi's decision.

**Short slot times.** By local calendar day, slot times read '4:20 AM' today, 'tomorrow 4:20 AM', 'Sat 4:20 AM' within the week and 'Oct 26, 4:20 AM' otherwise, in Schedule, Build and the resting lines, with the time kept on one line at 360 px (Avi's decision, 2026-10-20).
