# Evening Arena crowd

**Status.** Avi chose a small rotating crowd for every evening. Placement and art are ready for review in a clickable preview. This is a separate visual update after 1.8.

Four decorative townsfolk stand along the rear and outer sides of the Arena, behind the regular spectator positions. They face the ring and leave the front gate and walking paths clear. The arrangement changes with the game day and stays consistent within an evening, including on reload. They gather in the evening even when a bout is idle.

The preview compares the current camp with two evening arrangements using a real tournament spectator capture from `7804b1e` and the shipped civilian sprites. The local artifact is `logs/arena-evening-crowd-mockup-20261108/index.html` in the parent project folder. Desktop mouse and keyboard controls and mobile touch and zoom passed in installed Chrome. The examples use the water carrier, healer, cook, porter and armorer. Their exact mix and placement are proposed.
