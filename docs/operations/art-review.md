# Art review: Task 11 production pixel art

Reviewed 2026-10-12 by Claude, for the visual track on branch `claude/visual-track`. The art is
compared against approved reference panels A: `docs/superpowers/specs/references/art-style-comparison.png`
and `world-perspective-comparison.png`.

## Production route

Avi approved original pixel art written as deterministic code on 2026-10-12, after seeing a
rendered sample scene. This replaces the plan's "image-generation tool or free licensed packs".
Codex accepted the same route on the message board. It is reversible: if review finds the quality
insufficient, Codex can generate replacement sources with an image tool. Every shipped file is
listed in `assets/source/provenance.json` with its sha256, generator and license. `--check`
proves that the files rebuild byte-identically.

## Fixed during review

1. The figure was too thin. The rig became stockier: bigger head, broader shoulders, thicker limbs.
2. In idle and walk, the sword crossed the face. Weapon angles were changed so the blade stays at
   the body's edge.
3. Hair read as a helmet. The hair volume was reduced and the top tuft removed.
4. The oak canopy floated above its trunk. The trunk was lengthened and the canopy lowered.
5. The watchtower roof had no posts. Posts were added.
6. The forest outside the palisade was never placed, because its tiles were pre-blocked. Groves
   were also added to the empty grass quadrants.
7. Arena gate posts, torches and a crate blocked the plaza walkway. The ring stations were too close
   to the wall. The outpost camp tent stood on the route. All were moved.
8. The smith's black beard on dark skin read as a mask, and the trainer's gray hair looked like a
   helmet. Both got lighter beards. The independent review below then gave them distinct heads:
   the smith wears a leather cap, and the trainer has black hair and a gold headband.

## Deferred to Task 14: close-up battle view

Reference panel A shows battles as a close-up. Task 11 ships no close-up assets. Task 14 (battle
replay, this lane) accepts it only when:

1. A close-up arena backdrop (sand floor, wall arc, crowd or banners) is generated by the same
   code pipeline, listed in `provenance.json` and covered by `--check`.
2. Fighters reuse the gladiator and creature atlases with their layers and tints, drawn at an
   integer 3× scale (no fractional scaling of the art).
3. Two fighters face each other (SE and SW), stay fully inside the frame for every action, and
   effects land on the defender.
4. It stays readable at 360px wide; desktop and mobile screenshots are recorded with the report.

## Known limitations

- Skin variety comes from a multiply tint over one light base body (five tones at runtime).
- NE frames exist only for idle and walk. Activities and combat face SE or SW, matching how
  stations and fights are laid out (partners face each other horizontally on screen).
- The 60 fps desktop and 30 fps mobile targets need real-device measurement (Task 23).
