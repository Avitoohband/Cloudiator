# Offline balance simulations for 2.0

Generated 2026-11-12 by `node scripts/balance/run-sims.ts` (2000 fights per matchup). Nothing here changes a game number; the 2.0 patch decides what to do with it.

## Method

- Rules v1 combat (the Arena's and the expedition's use the same constants), classless fighters from `makeFighter`: attributes are base 5 plus the points, gear comes straight from the catalog, so gear and points are isolated. Balanced stance. Arena rules unless the Context column says expedition.
- Points at a level are the Training cap, 10 + 4 per level (level 10 = 50 points). The Balanced split is 20% to each attribute, the leftover points going in the order Strength, Agility, Vitality, Endurance, Precision.
- The critical sweep keeps the total fixed at 50 points, so raising Precision lowers the other four; read it as concentration, not as critical chance alone.
- Each fight uses the seed `sim-<scenario>-<matchup>-<n>`. The listed first side alternates every fight (half the fights each), so the first-attacker order cancels out.
- Win rate is the first named build's wins out of 2000; the interval is the Wilson 95% interval. Hit rate is the share of that build's attacks that landed; critical share is the part of its dealt damage that came from critical hits.

## What this means for 2.0

- Critical chance: the win rate falls steadily as points move into Precision (72.4%, 49.3%, 19.9%, 4.2%, 0.5%, 0.0% for Precision 0 to 50). The sweep takes those points out of the other four attributes, so it measures concentrating points in Precision, not critical chance on its own (the Precision 10 row is the identical build, so about 50% is expected). Critical chance only goes from 6.5% to 21.5% (the cap is 40%) and critical hits supply 9.6% to 30.2% of the damage dealt, while the hit rate rises from 68.2% to 93.0%. In this sweep, concentrating points in Precision does not pay.
- Two-handed weapons: the Spear averages 29.3% and the Bow 31.6% against one-handed weapons with a Round shield. The lowest result is Spear vs Sword + Round shield at level 10 (13.0%), the highest Bow vs Axe + Round shield at level 1 (58.1%). No two-handed matchup is above 60%, and every one against the Sword is below 35%; the Axe is the weaker one-handed opponent.
- Renown weapons: against the same-tier common sword they win 51.0%, 62.9%, 64.2% in the Arena (levels 1, 5, 10) and 50.1%, 47.9%, 48.9% on expeditions. The tier-5 renown sword against the tier-10 common sword at level 10 wins 37.5%.
- These are plain results from the current numbers only. No number change is proposed here; classless fighters leave out the class bonus, and real builds add it.
