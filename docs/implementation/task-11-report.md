## Production route

Original pixel art written as deterministic code (route C). Avi approved it on 2026-10-12 after seeing a rendered sample scene, and Codex accepted it on the board.
- The generators in `assets/source/generators/` render every runtime frame using a pose rig, palette-locked painters and seeded randomness.
- `node assets/source/build-assets.mjs` writes `public/assets/`. `--check` proves the build regenerates byte-identically. It reuses a committed PNG when its decoded pixels are unchanged, so zlib versions do not matter, and it reports orphan files.
- Every shipped file has a provenance record (sha256, generator inputs, license: project-original) in `assets/source/provenance.json`. No downloads, paid assets or image tools were used.

## Delivered assets

- **Atlases:** 10 atlases, 1,719 frames, 116 animations. Phaser JSON-hash frames carry a normalized `pivot` plus `anchorPx`. Actor frames are 64×64 with the anchor at (32, 58); tiles are 64×32 with the anchor at (32, 0).
- **Gladiators:** 17 animations: the 15 required plus spear `thrust` and bow `shoot`. Idle and walk are drawn SE and NE; the others are SE, mirrored for SW.
  - 15 layers:
    - body (skin tint), cloth, hair, light and heavy armor;
    - accent (cloth tint), hood, helmet, helmet crest (cloth tint);
    - sword, axe, spear and bow; shield; held props.
  - Every layer has a `tint` key and a slot order.
- **NPCs:** six, with distinct looks (smith, armorer, trainer, cook, healer, guard). Each has idle, walk (SE and NE), talk and tend.
- **Creatures:** two per expedition (dire and timber wolf, raider and raider archer, rock and frost troll). Each has idle, walk, attack, hit, defend, evade, defeat and victory.
- **Effects and icons:** 8 combat effects and 37 interface icons.
- **Maps:** four.
  - The 46×46 camp has zones, stations (16 training, 26 rest, 34 arena, 11 merchants including NPC posts, and 4 at the expedition gate), lanes, the palisade, forest and the arena.
  - The woodland, outpost and mountain expeditions each have a route, three encounters with assigned creatures, search spots and a safe point.
  - Corner autotiles keep transitions seamless.
- **Manifest:** `public/assets/manifest.json` holds:
  - the projection and palette (74 colours, hard alpha);
  - actions and directions;
  - atlases and animations;
  - actors, including `walk.tilesPerSecond: 1`;
  - `equipmentLayers` (catalog template id to layer);
  - `depthRule`;
  - effects, icons, maps (`mapFiles`) and provenance.
- **Runtime loader:** `src/game/assets.ts` deep-freezes the manifest and validates frame bounds, provenance, animations and map consistency and connectivity.
- **Depth rule:** in `src/game/depth.ts`, mirrored by `assets/source/tools/depth.mjs` for the offline renderers (tested equal).
  - Actors sort at their feet.
  - Wide props draw as 32px front-edge slices, and arena wall segments ship slices computed from the ring geometry.
  - Slices sort half a pixel early, so actors win ties.
- **Gallery and review:** `public/assets/gallery.html` plays every loop at the real durations, in SE, SW, NE and NW, with layer toggles and skin, cloth and hair tints. Its provenance record is hand-written.

## Limitations

- **Deferred to Task 14: the close-up battle view (reference panel A).** Task 11 ships no close-up assets. Task 14 is accepted only when:
  1. a close-up arena backdrop is generated by this pipeline, with provenance and `--check` coverage;
  2. fighters reuse these atlases, layers and tints at an integer 3× scale;
  3. two fighters face each other (SE and SW), stay inside the frame for every action, and effects land on the defender;
  4. it reads at 360px wide, with desktop and mobile screenshots recorded.
- NE frames exist only for idle and walk. Activities and combat face SE or SW, matching how stations and fights are laid out.
- Skin variety is a multiply tint over one light base body (five tones). Boots (`feet-light`, `feet-heavy`) have no layer yet.
- The smith's beard is still a hard light block when viewed at 8×. It reads correctly at game scale, and the reviewer accepted it as a Minor.
- The 60 fps desktop and 30 fps mobile targets need real-device measurement (Task 23).
