# Wiki changes to make when 1.9 ships

The Wiki explains live features only, so none of this goes into `wiki.draft.json` yet. When 1.9 is released, update the topics below and edit the draft through the in-game editor or the draft-only workflow. Text is in player words, ready to paste. Check each topic's current title and id in `wiki.draft.json` first, and drop a topic if 1.9 does not ship that feature.

## Shops (shops topic)

Each shop shows its goods as a grid of icons. Select an icon to see its card with the price, stats, and weight, then buy it. Use the filters for Weapons, Armor, and the gold or renown view. Click the Weaponsmith's or Armorer's stall in camp to open the shop on that filter. The Arena banner is sold by the Armorer and shows in the Renown view.

## Search and sort (inventory and shops topics)

Type in the search box to find an item by name. Use Sort to put items in the order you want, such as by level or by name. Both work in Inventory and in the shops.

## Stacks and Sell quantity (inventory topic)

Identical items that you are not using show as one tile with a count, such as x3. Each item is still its own copy: Wear and Discard work on one at a time. When you sell, choose how many to sell, or press Max. Worn, listed, and queued items keep their own tiles with a tag so you can tell them apart.

## Equip from My gladiator (My gladiator topic)

Under Worn equipment, select a slot to see the gear you could put there and pick a replacement. Identical copies appear as one stack.

## XP bar and next-expedition hint (My gladiator and expeditions topics)

The XP bar sits under Stamina and a thin XP line shows in the corner chip. A lighter segment shows what your next expedition could add. If it says the expedition can level you up, finishing it may take you to the next level. The hint uses your scheduled expedition, or the best one available if none is scheduled. It shows an upper estimate ("up to").

## Stat and attribute help (My gladiator topic)

My gladiator has Attributes and Stats blocks. Hover, focus, or tap an attribute or stat to read what it means and what changes it.

## Mark as read (messages and notifications topic)

Press the tick on a message to mark it read, or Mark all to clear every message. Notifications have a Mark all button. Opening the Notifications tab still marks them as seen. The button clears the dots.

## Menu pin and position (interface topic)

On desktop, open Settings (⚙) to pin the menu and choose whether it sits on the left, on the right, or along the bottom. Your choice is saved in this browser only, so another device can differ.

## Names and titles (camp and interface topics)

In Settings, under Names and titles, choose when names and titles show over the camp: Off, When zoomed in, or Always. When zoomed in shows gladiators' names from zoom 1.4 and NPC names and titles from zoom 2, and your selected gladiator's always. Always shows them at any zoom, even zoomed out. Where names overlap, the nearer one stays and the ones behind fade; point at a gladiator to bring its name back. Your choice is saved in this browser and works on phones too.

## Talking NPCs (camp topic)

Click or tap an NPC in camp to hear a line from them, and again for their next one. Each has four. Brutus and Livia talk too; click their stalls to open the shops.

## Sleeping (camp topic)

A resting gladiator lies straight on their bed with an eye cover, and a small ZzZ floats above them. It stops when they get up. In bed they take off their armor and helmet; they put them back on when they get up.

## Combat (critical damage and the Arena)

A critical hit deals 50% more damage than a normal hit.

In the Arena and tournaments, renown armor counts 10% more and a renown weapon deals 10% more.
